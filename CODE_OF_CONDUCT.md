# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make the D&D Mapp community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We foster an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual. That holds regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, or disability. It holds regardless of sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. Everyone who takes part in good faith and follows this code of conduct has the same privileges of participation.

## Encouraged behaviors

We all strive to meet the community's expectations for positive behavior, while acknowledging that social norms differ. Others may read our words and actions differently than we intend, because of culture, background, or native language. With that in mind, we behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that center our shared values:

1. Respecting the **purpose of the community**, its activities, and its ways of gathering.
2. Engaging **kindly and honestly** with others.
3. Respecting **different viewpoints** and experiences.
4. **Taking responsibility** for our actions and contributions.
5. Giving and accepting **constructive feedback** gracefully.
6. Committing to **repairing harm** when it occurs.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the **well-being of the community**.

## Restricted behaviors

The community does not allow the following behaviors. Committing, threatening, or promoting any of them violates this code of conduct.

1. **Harassment.** Violating boundaries someone has stated explicitly, or giving unnecessary personal attention after a clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks.** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a community member or group of people.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterizing anyone's personality or behavior based on immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualization.** Behaving in a way generally considered inappropriately intimate for the context or purpose of the community.
5. **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or private information without their permission.
6. **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
7. Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of the community.

### Other restrictions

1. **Misleading identity.** Impersonating someone else for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement.
2. **Failing to credit sources.** Not properly crediting the sources of content you contribute.
3. **Promotional materials.** Sharing marketing or other commercial content outside the norms of the community.
4. **Irresponsible communication.** Presenting content that includes, links to, or describes any other restricted behavior without due care.

## Reporting a violation

Tensions can arise between community members even when they try their best to collaborate. Not every conflict violates this code of conduct, and the behaviors it encourages help avoid conflicts and minimize harm.

When an incident does occur, report it promptly by email to [conduct@dndmapp.nl.eu.org](mailto:conduct@dndmapp.nl.eu.org). The [lead maintainer](GOVERNANCE.md#lead-maintainer) reads every report and keeps it private. When your report concerns the lead maintainer, you can also [report the content to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The lead maintainer takes every report seriously and responds as soon as they can, though D&D Mapp is a volunteer project and promises no response time. They investigate every report, which can include reviewing messages and logs and talking to witnesses and other participants. They keep investigations and enforcement as transparent as possible while putting safety and confidentiality first. Enforcement therefore happens in private with the people involved, though a resolution they agree on can include telling the whole community.

## Addressing and repairing harm

When an investigation finds that someone violated this code of conduct, the lead maintainer uses the steps below to repair the harm. The step depends on the impact on the people involved and on the community as a whole, and a severe violation can skip the lower steps.

### 1. Warning

- **Event:** A violation involving a single incident or a series of incidents.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning from the lead maintainer.
- **Repair:** For example, a private written apology, acknowledging responsibility, and asking for clarity on expectations.

### 2. Temporarily limited activities

- **Event:** A repeat of a violation that already led to a warning, or a first, more serious violation.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning with a cooldown period that underscores how serious the situation is and gives the people involved time to process the incident. The cooldown can apply to particular channels or to interactions with particular community members.
- **Repair:** For example, apologizing, using the cooldown to reflect on your actions and their impact, and returning to community spaces thoughtfully once it ends.

### 3. Temporary suspension

- **Event:** A pattern of violations that warnings have not stopped, or a single serious violation.
- **Consequence:** A private, written warning with the conditions for returning from the suspension. The suspension gives the person time to reflect on their behavior and how to correct it.
- **Repair:** For example, respecting the spirit of the suspension, meeting the conditions for return, and reintegrating with the community thoughtfully once it is lifted.

### 4. Permanent ban

- **Event:** A pattern of violations that the other steps have failed to resolve, or a violation so serious that the lead maintainer sees no way to keep the community safe with this person in it.
- **Consequence:** Removal of access to all community spaces, tools, and communication channels. A permanent ban is a last resort and needs strong reasons.
- **Repair:** None is possible at this severity.

These steps are a guideline. They do not limit the lead maintainer's discretion and judgment in the best interests of the community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every space of the [dnd-mapp GitHub organization](https://github.com/dnd-mapp): the [repository](https://github.com/dnd-mapp/dnd-mapp), its issues, pull requests, and [Discussions](https://github.com/dnd-mapp/dnd-mapp/discussions), and the [D&D Mapp project](https://github.com/orgs/dnd-mapp/projects/10). It also applies to anyone officially representing D&D Mapp in public, such as by using an official email address, posting from an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
