# Card

A block of related content, alone or in a grid, such as a tool on the landing page. It is the Card component of the design system in Penpot.

## Placing a card

Card is an element that projects a heading as its title and a `<p>` as its text:

```html
<dma-card>
    <h3>Characters</h3>
    <p>Build character sheets and keep their spell books.</p>
</dma-card>
```

Pick the heading level that fits the outline of the page, from `<h1>` to `<h6>`. The title draws the same at every level. Both parts are optional, and a card without text leaves no gap below its title. The card drops any other content.

The card fills its container, such as a column of a grid, and stays at least 280 px wide, so a grid wraps to fewer columns rather than squeezing them.

## Background

`background` picks what the card fills itself with, by what it sits on, so the card always stands one step above its background. An unknown value draws the default.

### Surface

`CardBackground.Surface`, the default, is for a card on the page background, as the card at the top of this page shows.

### Raised

`CardBackground.Raised` is for a card on a surface, such as a section that fills itself with the surface background.

```html
<dma-card background="raised">
    <h3>Characters</h3>
    <p>Build character sheets and keep their spell books.</p>
</dma-card>
```

## Focus

A card takes no focus, and Penpot draws no hover or focus state for it, since it is not clickable as a whole.

## Accessibility

Assistive technology reads the title at the level you pick, then the text. The card adds no role of its own.

## Testing

`CardHarness` from `@dnd-mapp/ui-web/testing` reads a card in a test: the text, look, and place of its title and text, the look of its box, its size, and whether it has focus.
